My Creative Side

Creativity is a very important part of who I am. Outside of school and career goals, I enjoy floral design, event styling, decorating, and creating beautiful experiences for my friends and family.

I love working with my hands and creating things that feel intentional, elegant, and meaningful. Flowers allow me to express color, emotion, and personality. Event planning allows me to bring people together and create memories.

I also enjoy sharing parts of my lifestyle and creativity on social media. Today, media can help people build confidence, share their talents, and create future opportunities. I use TikTok as a way to express my creative side and grow my personal brand.
