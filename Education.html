My Academic Journey

My education started in Angola, where I attended high school from 2015 to 2017 and studied drilling and production of oil. This was the beginning of my interest in petroleum, energy, and the oil and gas industry.

When I moved to the United States in 2018, I had to adjust to a new country, a new language, and a new education system. I started at Houston Community College, where I spent my first years learning English and building the foundation I needed to continue college successfully.

After completing my English studies, I earned an Associate of Arts in Business. I later continued with Petroleum Engineering coursework at HCC while also pursuing my bachelor’s degree in Business Data Analytics at Arizona State University.

I chose Business Data Analytics because it allows me to combine technical thinking, business decision-making, and data. My goal is to work in the oil and gas industry in a business or analytics role, where I can use data to support better decisions.
