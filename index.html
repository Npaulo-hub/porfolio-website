About Me

My name is Nelma Paulo. I was born in Luanda, Angola, on March 25. I moved to the United States in 2018 to pursue better educational and professional opportunities. Coming from Angola gave me strong cultural values, discipline, and a deep appreciation for family, education, and resilience.

I am the third child in a family of five children. My father is a doctor and my mother is a nurse, and they have always been an inspiration to me. Their work ethic, dedication, and support have motivated me to keep growing academically and professionally.

As a Black woman who moved to a new country with big expectations, I have had to adapt, learn, and develop new skills. I moved to the United States without knowing English, but I continued pushing forward because I knew education would open doors for my future.

My Professional Direction

I am currently two semesters away from completing my bachelor’s degree in Business Data Analytics at Arizona State University. I also hold an Associate of Arts in Business and am completing another associate degree in Petroleum Engineering at Houston Community College.

My long-term goal is to work in the oil and gas industry, but from the business and analytics side. I have always loved petroleum engineering, but after moving to the United States, I discovered how many opportunities exist in business, strategy, and data. That is why I decided to combine my background in petroleum with business analytics.

This portfolio represents who I am: a student, a future analytics professional, a woman with roots in Angola, and a creative person who enjoys flowers, events, and meaningful design.
